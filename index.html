Step File Tube Notcher

MASSO G3 plasma rotary. Cut so the ID sits on the parent OD. Zero the far end (End 1): seam is A0 and Y0. End 2 is negative Y at the chuck. Cut far end first so the pipe does not drop. Pierce on the path — no lead-in or lead-out. Preloaded: cage24 back angle bar.

How to use

  1. Drop the STEP of the round tube you want to cut. Not the whole chassis — just that bar.
  2. The notcher reads OD, wall, length, angle, and the OD of the two pipes each end sits on. G-code builds itself.
  3. Glance at the numbers. If the chassis tube is 1.5 in and STEP says 1.528, type 1.5 in “Sits on pipe OD”.
  4. Download .nc. MASSO: T112, zero the far end (End 1) at Y0, seam is A0. Chuck is End 2. Cut far end first so the pipe does not drop.

Preloaded from back angle bar1.step — 1.5 in × 0.083 wall, sits on 1.5 in both ends at 76.45°, 29.873 in reference-to-reference.
